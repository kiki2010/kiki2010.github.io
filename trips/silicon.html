Landing, finally.

After months of preparation, we finally landed — and the second I stepped outside it hit me that this trip was actually happening. That mix of exhaustion and pure excitement you only get when something you've worked so hard for finally starts.

We didn't do much that first night besides settle in and let it sink in: we were really here, about to spend the next few days living something we'd remember forever.

Surrounded by everything I love.

I was genuinely so happy this day. Walking into EA and being surrounded by videogames, screens, and people who spend their lives building videogames. We got to play FIFA, a Star Wars game, and The Sims right there in their offices.

Between games we toured the facilities and sat down for pitch practice, but what stayed with me most was that feeling of being somewhere I truly belonged, even if just for a day.
